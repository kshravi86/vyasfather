Absolutely—here’s the clean, standard Jaimini way to compute Arūḍha Padas (A1–A12) for all 12 bhāvas, including the one widely-accepted exception.

# What an Arūḍha (Pada) is

For any house (bhāva), its Arūḍha (or Pada) is the “manifest image” of that house. In Jaimini practice this is **rāśi-based** (whole-sign), not cusp/degree based.

# The standard method (use this for each of the 12 houses)

1. **Use whole-sign houses.**
   The 1st house is the Lagna sign; the 2nd is the next sign, etc.

2. **Find the lord of the house sign.**
   Use classical rulers only:

   * Aries/Scorpio → Mars
   * Taurus/Libra → Venus
   * Gemini/Virgo → Mercury
   * Cancer → Moon
   * Leo → Sun
   * Sagittarius/Pisces → Jupiter
   * Capricorn/Aquarius → Saturn
     (Rāhu/Keṭu are **not** used as sign lords for this calculation.)

3. **Count the distance from the house to its lord’s sign (inclusive, forward through the zodiac).**
   Inclusive means: house to itself = 1; to the next sign = 2; … to the opposite sign = 7, etc.
   Let this count be **N**.

4. **From the lord’s sign, count forward the same N (inclusive).**
   The sign you land in is the **Arūḍha (Pada)** of that house.

5. **Exception (the only widely-followed correction).**
   If the **lord is in the same sign as the house (N = 1)** **or** **in the 7th from that house (N = 7)**, then **do not** place the Pada by step 4.
   Instead, place the Pada **10th from the lord’s sign**.

   * If lord in the **same** sign: Pada goes to **10th from that house**.
   * If lord in the **7th**: Pada goes to **4th from that house** (because “10th from the 7th” = 4th).

> Notes on practice:
>
> * This exception is applied **only** when the **lord** is in the **1st or 7th** from the house. If the computed Pada (by step 4) happens to fall in the house itself or its 7th for other lord placements (e.g., lord in 4th/10th), most lineages **keep it as is**.
> * Retrogression, combustion, aspects, etc., **do not** change the counting.
> * Always count **signs**, not degrees.

# Names (mapping A1–A12)

* **A1** = Arūḍha Lagna (AL) — Pada of the 1st house
* **A2** = Dhana Pada — Pada of the 2nd house
* **A3** = Bhrātṛ Pada — Pada of the 3rd
* **A4** = Mātṛ Pada — Pada of the 4th
* **A5** = Mantra/Putra Pada — Pada of the 5th
* **A6** = Śatru/Roga Pada — Pada of the 6th
* **A7** = Dāra/Yuvati (Dāra Pada) — Pada of the 7th
* **A8** = Randhra Pada — Pada of the 8th
* **A9** = Bhāgya Pada — Pada of the 9th
* **A10** = Karma Pada — Pada of the 10th
* **A11** = Lābha Pada — Pada of the 11th
* **A12** = Vyaya Pada — Pada of the 12th

  > Special name: **Upapada (UL)** is specifically the Pada of the **12th house**; you compute it by the same steps and the same exception rule.

# Quick worked examples (to make it concrete)

**Example 1 — No exception (A1):**
Lagna = **Leo**. Sun (lord of Leo) in **Sagittarius**.
Distance Leo→Sagittarius (inclusive): **5**.
Count 5 from Sagittarius (inclusive): Sagittarius (1), Capricorn (2), Aquarius (3), Pisces (4), **Aries (5)**.
**AL = Aries.**

**Example 2 — Exception: lord in the same sign (A1):**
Lagna = **Taurus**. Venus (lord) in **Taurus** (N = 1).
Use exception → **10th from the lord’s sign** (Taurus):
10th from Taurus = **Aquarius**.
**AL = Aquarius.**

**Example 3 — Exception: lord in the 7th (A1):**
Lagna = **Gemini**. Mercury (lord) in **Sagittarius** (7th from Gemini, N = 7).
Use exception → **10th from Sagittarius** = **Virgo** (which is 4th from Gemini).
**AL = Virgo.**

**Example 4 — A2 (same method on another house):**
1st = **Cancer** ⇒ 2nd house sign = **Leo**.
Lord of Leo = Sun, suppose Sun is in **Libra**.
Distance Leo→Libra: **3**.
Count 3 from Libra: Libra(1), Scorpio(2), **Sagittarius(3)**.
**A2 = Sagittarius** (no exception, since lord is neither in Leo nor Aquarius).

# Minimal pseudocode (if you want to compute programmatically)

```text
Given HOUSE_SIGN:
  LORD_SIGN = classical_ruler(HOUSE_SIGN) → sign where that planet sits
  N = inclusive_distance(HOUSE_SIGN → LORD_SIGN)  # 1..12

  if N == 1 or N == 7:
      PADA = tenth_from(LORD_SIGN)
  else:
      PADA = count_inclusive(LORD_SIGN, N)

Return PADA
```

# Common pitfalls to avoid

* Don’t use quadrant/cusp houses; **use whole-sign** bhāvas.
* Don’t swap to modern co-rulerships; **use classical lords** only.
* Don’t reverse count for retrograde planets; the count is always **forward** and **inclusive**.
* Don’t apply extra “corrections” beyond the **single exception** above unless you follow a lineage that explicitly instructs otherwise.
