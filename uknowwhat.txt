You are correct to ask for the specific calculation "as per Meena 2 Nadi," as its definition of **Uttama Drekkana** is a unique and foundational concept of that system, different from the standard Parashari method.

In the Meena 2 Nadi system (as taught by Sri N.V. Raghava Chary and Dr. NVRA Raja), Uttama Drekkana is not based on the *lordship* of the Drekkana (like Swa-Drekkana). Instead, it is a *specific 10-degree portion of a sign* that is considered most auspicious, based on the sign's modality (Movable, Fixed, or Dual).

A planet in this specific "Uttama" (best) 10-degree slice is considered to have special strength, giving it the power to deliver excellent results, even if it is otherwise afflicted or debilitated.

### The Meena 2 Nadi Calculation

The calculation is based on the **modality of the sign** (Rasi) the planet is in.

* **1. Chara Rasis (Movable Signs):** Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn
    * The **1st Drekkana** (0° 00' to 10° 00') is the Uttama Drekkana.

* **2. Sthira Rasis (Fixed Signs):** Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius
    * The **2nd Drekkana** (10° 01' to 20° 00') is the Uttama Drekkana.

* **3. Dwiswabhava Rasis (Dual Signs):** Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces
    * The **3rd Drekkana** (20° 01' to 30° 00') is the Uttama Drekkana.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Check a Planet

Follow these simple steps:

**Step 1: Identify the planet's sign and degree.**
* *Example:* You want to check **Venus at 24° Virgo**.

**Step 2: Identify the sign's modality (Movable, Fixed, or Dual).**
* *Example:* Virgo is a **Dual (Dwiswabhava)** sign.

**Step 3: Find the Uttama Drekkana for that modality.**
* *Example:* For Dual signs, the Uttama Drekkana is the **3rd Drekkana** (20° 01' to 30° 00').

**Step 4: Check if the planet's degree falls within that range.**
* *Example:* Venus is at **24°**. This degree *is* within the 20° 01' to 30° 00' range.
* **Result:** Venus at 24° Virgo **is** in Uttama Drekkana.

### More Examples

**Example 1: Sun at 5° Aries**
1.  **Planet:** Sun at 5° Aries.
2.  **Sign Modality:** Aries is a **Movable (Chara)** sign.
3.  **Uttama Range:** For Movable signs, the Uttama Drekkana is the **1st Drekkana** (0° 00' to 10° 00').
4.  **Check:** The Sun's degree of 5° falls within this range.
    * **Result:** Sun **is** in Uttama Drekkana.

**Example 2: Jupiter at 18° Leo**
1.  **Planet:** Jupiter at 18° Leo.
2.  **Sign Modality:** Leo is a **Fixed (Sthira)** sign.
3.  **Uttama Range:** For Fixed signs, the Uttama Drekkana is the **2nd Drekkana** (10° 01' to 20° 00').
4.  **Check:** Jupiter's degree of 18° falls within this range.
    * **Result:** Jupiter **is** in Uttama Drekkana.

**Example 3: Mars at 12° Cancer**
1.  **Planet:** Mars at 12° Cancer (note: Mars is debilitated in Cancer).
2.  **Sign Modality:** Cancer is a **Movable (Chara)** sign.
3.  **Uttama Range:** For Movable signs, the Uttama Drekkana is the **1st Drekkana** (0° 00' to 10° 00').
4.  **Check:** Mars's degree of 12° is *outside* this range.
    * **Result:** Mars is **not** in Uttama Drekkana.
