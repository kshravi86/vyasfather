here’s the single, widely-followed Parāśari method (what most software like JHora/PL uses) to compute the Saptāṁśa (D7).

# Saptāṁśa (D7) — standard calculation

1. **Use sidereal longitudes**

   * Pick an ayanāṁśa (most use **Lahiri/Chitra**).
   * Convert each planet’s tropical longitude to **sidereal**: 
     `λ_sidereal = λ_tropical − ayanāṁśa`.

2. **Find the sign and in-sign position**

   * Sign index `s = floor(λ_sidereal / 30)`  (0=Aries, …, 11=Pisces).
   * In-sign degrees `δ = λ_sidereal − 30*s`  (so `0° ≤ δ < 30°`).

3. **Split each sign into 7 equal parts**

   * One part length `L = 30°/7 = 4°17′08.571″`.
   * Saptāṁśa part number `k = floor( δ / L )` → `k ∈ {0,…,6}`.
     (Treat the **lower bound inclusive, upper bound exclusive**: e.g., 4°17′08.571″ belongs to the next part.)

4. **Assign the D7 sign (odd/even rule)**

   * If the natal sign is **odd** (Ar, Ge, Le, Li, Sg, Aq):
     the **1st** D7 part starts from the **same sign**; then proceed zodiacally.
     `D7_sign_index = (s + k) mod 12`.
   * If the natal sign is **even** (Ta, Cn, Vi, Sc, Cp, Pi):
     the **1st** D7 part starts from the **7th sign from it**, then proceed zodiacally.
     `D7_sign_index = (s + 6 + k) mod 12`.

   Quick “start sign” lookup for even signs:

   * Taurus→Scorpio, Cancer→Capricorn, Virgo→Pisces,
     Scorpio→Taurus, Capricorn→Cancer, Pisces→Virgo.

5. **Degrees inside the D7 sign (optional but standard)**

   * Remainder inside the selected part: `ρ = δ − k*L` (so `0° ≤ ρ < L`).
   * Map to full 30° span of the D7 sign: **`D7_degrees = ρ × 7`**.
   * Final D7 longitude = `30*D7_sign_index + D7_degrees`.

6. **Houses in D7**

   * Cast the D7 chart with the **same reference (Lagna) method** you use for other vargas (most simply place planets in their D7 signs; many keep the D7 Lagna as the D1 Lagna’s D7 placement).

7. **Edge cases**

   * Exactly on a boundary (e.g., 0°, L, 2L, …): assign to the **new** part starting at that boundary (lower-inclusive / upper-exclusive rule keeps things unambiguous).
   * Retrograde doesn’t change the calculation—use longitude only.

---

## Worked example (one planet)

* Planet: **15°20′ Cancer** (sidereal).
* `s = Cancer = 3` (Ar=0, Ta=1, Ge=2, **Cn=3**).
* `δ = 15°20′ = 15.333…°`.
* `L = 4.285714…°`.
* `k = floor(15.333 / 4.285714) = 3` → 4th D7 part.
* Cancer is **even** → start from **Capricorn** (7th from Cancer).
  `D7_sign_index = 3 + 6 + 3 = 12 ≡ 0 (Aries)`.
  ⇒ **D7 sign = Aries**.
* Remainder `ρ = 15.333 − 3×4.285714 = 2.4759°`.
* `D7_degrees = 2.4759 × 7 = 17.33°` ≈ **17°20′**.
* **Result**: Planet sits at **Aries 17°20′** in the Saptāṁśa.
